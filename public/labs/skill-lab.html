Inside the lab

What you do in here

Every track runs the same way: build, review, ship, repeat. Nothing here is watched passively.

🚀

Project sprints

Two-week builds with a real deliverable at the end. You learn the tool by needing it, not by watching it.

🔍

Mentor code & design reviews

A working professional reviews what you built, line by line — the feedback that actually moves you.

💼

A portfolio, not a certificate

Every sprint ends in something you can send to an employer. By track's end you have three to five pieces.

🗺️

Skills mapped to real jobs

Each track is reverse-engineered from live job postings, so what you learn is what's being hired for.

🤖

Exzi as your pair

Stuck at midnight? Your AI companion knows the project you're on and explains without doing it for you.

👥

Build in public

Ship alongside a cohort. Accountability, code reviews and the odd rescue when you're stuck.
