How it works

From knowing better to doing better

Name the block

One honest conversation about what's actually in the way. Usually it isn't what you think.

Pick one change

One programme, one habit, one direction. Doing five things is how nothing changes.

Run the week

Small daily actions, tracked. Exzi checks in; your coach and group hold the line.

Review honestly

Every week: what moved, what didn't, what changes. Repeat until it's just who you are.
